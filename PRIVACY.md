# Privacy Policy

Live Wallpaper Dashboard is a Windows app made by Rabiya Tahir. This page explains what the app does with your information.

*Last updated: 25 September 2026*

## The short version

- The app has no servers of its own and no accounts. It doesn't collect analytics or show ads.
- Your settings, alarms, reminders and wallpaper are stored only on your computer.
- If you connect Google Calendar, the app talks directly to Google from your computer. Your calendar data is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

## Information stored on your computer

The app keeps the following in its folder on your computer (`%APPDATA%\Live Wallpaper Dashboard`):

- your wallpaper picture, widget layout and colors
- your alarms, alarm sounds you added, and reminders
- links to other calendars you added (iCal links)
- if you signed in to Google: your Google account's email address and a sign-in token, encrypted by Windows so only your Windows account can read it

Nothing in this folder is uploaded anywhere. To remove it all, uninstall the app and then delete that folder.

## Google Calendar

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, the app asks Google for:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To show which account is connected |
| See your calendars and events (`calendar.readonly`) | To show your events on the dashboard |
| Add, change and delete events (`calendar.events`) | So you can add and edit events and their reminders from the app |

The app only uses this data on your computer, to show your events and make the changes you ask for. It does not store your events beyond a short-lived copy in memory, and it does not share, sell or transfer them to anyone. It never uses them for advertising, and no person reads them.

Live Wallpaper Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, press **Sign out** in the app's Calendars panel. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Other services the app uses

To work, the app sends some requests directly from your computer to these services. Each has its own privacy policy.

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) | Your approximate coordinates | Weather |
| [ipwho.is](https://ipwho.is) | Your IP address (as with any website) | Your approximate city, only if Windows Location is off |
| Wallhaven, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Openverse | What you type in the wallpaper search | Finding wallpapers |
| Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo | What you search in the "Search the whole web" window | Web image search you open yourself |
| GitHub | A check for new versions | Automatic updates |

Your exact location from Windows (if you turned it on) is used only to ask Open-Meteo for the weather.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
