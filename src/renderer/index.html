Other calendars (read-only link)

Show another calendar, such as a university timetable or Outlook, from its private iCal link. These can't be edited here.

Where do I find this link?

Google Calendar (on a computer): Settings → click your calendar under "Settings for my calendars" → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook / Microsoft 365: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → under "Publish a calendar" pick your calendar and Can view all details → Publish → copy the ICS link.

Apple iCloud: in the Calendar app, share the calendar as a Public Calendar and copy the link.

Keep this link private: anyone who has it can see your events. The app only reads it.
