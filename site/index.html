Mac-style desktop widgets, now on Windows

Your wallpaper becomes a live dashboard: the time, weather, your Google Calendar, alarms and reminders, right on your desktop, behind your icons.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

Free and open source. No account needed.

Everything you need, at a glance

Windows can't put widgets on your desktop by itself. This app can, and it takes a minute to set up.

Widgets you can click

Tick off a reminder, open a day on the calendar or change an alarm, right on your desktop. Your icons keep working as usual.

Your Google Calendar

See what's coming up, and add or edit events without opening a browser. Get reminder emails from Google.

Alarms that understand you

Type tomorrow 7am or every weekday 8:30. Pick a tone or your own song.

Any wallpaper, instantly

Search for a character, movie or place and pick a picture, or use your own. Widget colors match it automatically.

Weather and battery

Live weather for where you are, and your battery at a glance. One click opens the Windows Weather app or battery settings.

Updates itself

Download it once. New versions install quietly in the background.

Click, change, done

Click the bell, a date or an event on your desktop and just that pops up. Make your change, press Esc, and you're back on your desktop.

Make it yours

Move and resize every widget, hide the ones you don't need, and switch wallpapers in a click. Six built-in wallpapers look sharp on any screen.

Install in a minute

Download

Get the Setup .exe file from the latest release.

Run the installer

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because the app is new, not because anything is wrong.

Make it yours

Pick a wallpaper and connect your calendar. Find everything under the app's icon near the clock on the taskbar.
