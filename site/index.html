Why

Windows can't do this by itself

Macs let you put widgets on the desktop. Windows keeps them in a side panel that hides away. This app brings them to your desktop, where you can see them.

Features

Everything at a glance

Set up in a minute. No accounts, no settings to fiddle with.

Widgets you can click, right on the desktop

Tick off a reminder, open a day on the calendar or change an alarm without opening anything. Just that one thing pops up, and Esc takes you back. Your icons keep working as usual.

Your Google Calendar

See what's next, add and edit events, and get reminder emails from Google.

Alarms that understand you

Type it the way you'd say it. Pick a tone or your own song.

On your desktop
